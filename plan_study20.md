# plan_study20.md — The Ten-Year Plan at 20 Hours a Week (10,000 Hours)

> **What this is.** A correction and rescoping of `plan_study.md` for a budget of
> **20 hours per week over 520 weeks ≈ 10,400 hours**. It keeps that document's goal
> — read, understand, fix and shrink the whole of CNA by hand — and keeps its
> dependency order. What it changes is the *arithmetic*, because the original
> baseline undercounts the corpus by more than half.
>
> **Read `plan_study.md` first.** This document does not repeat its week tables. It
> states what was measured wrong, what the corrected numbers demand, and how the
> second ten hours a week are to be spent. Where this document and `plan_study.md`
> disagree, this one wins; everything it does not mention stands unchanged.

## 1. Why 10 h/week could not have worked, and 20 h/week can

The relevant number is not total hours, it is **sustained lines-per-reading-hour**.
Careful inspection of unfamiliar systems C++ against a reference runs at roughly
100–200 lines/hour. Above ~250 it is not verification, it is skimming.

**Original plan.** 480 working weeks × 4 h reading = **1,920 reading hours.**
Even reading *only* the deep tier defined in §2 below (445,246 lines) that is
**232 lines/hour sustained for a decade, with zero hours left** for `c-api`,
`sharp-runtime`, or the Tier B/C renderers. The plan's own stated pace of
"~870 lines per working week" was derived from the undercounted baseline; against
the real corpus the same schedule demands ~1,870 lines/week.

**This plan.** 460 working weeks × 7 h reading = **3,220 reading hours**, split
across three tiers that are *read differently* (§2):

| Tier | Lines | Share of reading budget | Effective rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 — deep, line-by-line, reference open | 445,246 | 80% (2,576 h) | **173 lines/h** ✅ |
| T2 — contract + machine verification | 298,052 | 15% (483 h) | not read line-by-line |
| T3 — skim to decide its fate | 124,485 | 5% (161 h) | 773 lines/h ✅ |

173 lines/hour is inside the honest inspection band. **This is the whole argument
for 20 hours: the second ten hours are not an upgrade, they are what makes the
first ten hours' promise true.**

## 2. The three tiers of corpus

The mistake to avoid is treating 867,783 lines as one undifferentiated reading
task. Three kinds of code are in there, and only one of them is read line by line.

### T1 — Deep read (445,246 lines)

Line by line, FNA reference open, the seven questions of `plan_study.md` §1.3
answered in the notes.

- All non-renderer CNA modules **except `c-api`**: 221,916 lines
  (`graphics` 47,102 · `content` 39,951 · `graphics-ext` 30,544 · `platform` 19,731 ·
  `math` 13,523 · `audio` 13,120 · `devices` 10,883 · `input` 10,172 ·
  `gamer-services` 9,086 · `media` 8,276 · `net` 7,572 · `runtime` 5,560 ·
  `core` 2,947 · `devices-ext` 1,614 · `storage` 938 · `video-ffmpeg` 897)
- Tier A renderers per `plan_study.md` §2: 223,330 lines
  (`common` 61,970 · `bgfx` 24,080 · `vulkan` 22,697 · `directx9` 15,504 ·
  `webgpu` 15,913 · `sdl-gpu` 14,862 · `easygl` 14,213 · `directx12` 8,549 ·
  `fna3d` 6,890 · `software` 6,058 · `opengl4` 5,766 · `directx11` 5,092 ·
  `opengl2` 4,327 · `html-dom` 3,697 · `svg-dom` 3,429 · `metal` 3,424 ·
  `opengl1` 1,920 · `headless` 1,824 · `canvas` 1,571 · `sdl-renderer` 1,255 ·
  `stub` 289)

`modules/renderers/common` at 61,970 lines is the largest single item in T1 and
the one every other renderer depends on. It is a *year-3* subject in
`plan_study.md` and must stay there.

### T2 — Contract and machine verification (298,052 lines, not read line by line)

Code whose correctness is established by a generator plus a round-trip test, not
by a human reading each line. Reading every line here is the single biggest way
to waste the decade.

**`modules/c-api` — 115,500 lines.** A C ABI binding over the whole C++ surface.
It is mechanical by construction and grows with every API change. What must be
understood is the *generator and the ABI contract*, then verified by a round-trip
test per binding — not 115,500 lines of marshalling. **Deliverable: the
enumeration of bound symbols is produced by a committed script, and the human
reads the script, not its output.**

**`sharp-runtime` — 182,552 lines across 1,051 public headers.** Measured today, CNA
includes **2** distinct `SharpRuntime/*` headers directly and references
**73 distinct `System::` types** in total. The studied surface is therefore
**73 types, not 1,051 headers.** The existing 13-week allocation (W40–W52) is
adequate *once it is scoped this way* and hopeless if scoped to the repository.
**Deliverable: the 73-type list is generated and committed in W40; the rest of
sharp-runtime is explicitly out of scope for the decade.**

### T3 — Skim to decide (124,485 lines)

Read only to answer *maintain / freeze / remove*. Never line by line.

- Tier B (`plan_study.md` §2): `sokol` 21,850 · `diligent` 7,066 · `magnum` 6,866 ·
  `direct2d` 4,154 · `opengles1` 3,695 · `portablegl` 2,629 · `gdi` 1,978 ·
  `freedirect` 1,633 = **49,871**
- Tier C: `llgl` 16,448 · `skia` 9,684 · `wicked` 5,935 · `glide` 5,053 ·
  `blend2d` 2,055 · `openvg` 1,573 · `directx1`–`8`,`10` 17,623 = **58,371**
- **Unclassified — assign a tier in W4:** `igl` 9,071 · `tinygl` 2,823 ·
  `pixijs` 2,104 · `nanovg` 2,245 = **16,243**

## 3. The weekly 20 hours: two tracks, one week apart

Do **not** spend the second ten hours reading more pages. Comprehension has a
per-week ceiling; doubling the pages past it produces notes, not understanding.
Spend them *doing* — which is also what the plan is for ("develop and improve by
hand"), and what turns reading into knowledge.

| Track | Block | h | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** Comprehension | A1 Reading | **7** | CNA next to the FNA reference. Never without it. |
| | A2 Running | **3** | Build, run, debugger, print. Code only read is code not understood. |
| **B** Production | B1 Intervention | **6** | Fixes, tests, simplifications — from **last week's** findings. |
| | B2 Measurement | **2** | Numbers before/after; a regression guard for what was fixed. |
| | B3 Writing | **2** | `study/W<NNN>-<topic>.md`, `known_bugs.md`, the commit. |
| | | **20** | |

**The one-week lag is the point.** Track B this week consumes what Track A found
*last* week. `plan_study.md` §1.5 already forbids refactoring a module you have
not read; the same reasoning forbids fixing what you read an hour ago. Sleeping
on a finding is what separates a fix from a reflex, and it means every
intervention starts from written notes rather than from a fresh impression.

This absorbs the ~60 weeks that `plan_study.md` spends on standalone "Fix #1 /
Commit" slots (W153–154, W219, W230, W294–296, W369–376, W406–413, W430–438,
W443–447, W463–466 and others). Those stop being Track A weeks and become the
continuous Track B stream. **That recovered time is what pays for the scope
`plan_study.md` omitted.**

### 3.1 Required output per week

Unchanged from `plan_study.md` §1.2, plus one line:

4. **The AI ledger entry** (§4): every commit carries `AI-Assisted: <n> lines`,
   `0` included. Un-ledgered is un-auditable.

## 4. AI policy: targeted means machine-verifiable

The rule is not "use AI a little." It is: **AI is allowed exactly where its output
is cheaper to verify than to produce, and forbidden where verifying it costs more
than doing it by hand.** Everything below follows from that one test.

### 🟢 Green — unrestricted

The deliverable is a **committed script**, never the model's prose. Verification is
re-running it.

- Measurement, inventories, corpus counts, coverage tables
- Cross-reference tables: which renderer implements which contract method; which
  doc claims what; which public API has no test
- Call-site enumeration, dead-code candidate lists
- Mechanical CNA↔FNA diffs (`scripts/compare-fna-reference.py` and successors)
- Test *inputs*: fuzz corpora, fixture assets, corrupted-XNB generators
- Build, CI and benchmark plumbing
- Formatting, include hygiene

### 🟡 Amber — assists, human owns the semantics

Hard cap **3 files per week**, and the human writes the expected value *first*.

- A test body where the human already wrote the assertion
- Doxygen drafted from FNA's XML docs onto a member the human has read
- Mechanical refactors with the compiler as oracle (rename, extract, constness)
- Implementing a fix the human has already diagnosed **and specified in the notes**

**Acceptance test: the human can explain every changed line with the model closed.**
If not, revert it. A line you cannot explain is a line you did not write, and this
is a plan about not having lines like that.

### 🔴 Red — forbidden for the decade

- Deciding whether a behavior is *correct*. That is the FNA/XNA reference's job,
  and a real XNA 4.0 runtime is available on this machine.
- Any change to a capability report, or any `✅` in `AUDIT.md`
- Closing a `known_bugs.md` entry
- New renderer, new module, new public API
- Anything in a module not yet read (`plan_study.md` §1.5, extended to tools)
- Any batch spanning more than 3 files

### 4.2 The budget

**≤ 200 AI-originated production lines committed per week** (92,000 over the
decade, 11% of the corpus), each covered by a human-written assertion. Tally the
`AI-Assisted:` trailers in every year-end reserve week. If the tally exceeds
budget, the following year is Amber-free.

> The original three-month generation mode is what produced a 685,231-line corpus
> nobody has read. The budget exists so that the cure cannot become the disease.

## 5. The rule that makes the decade terminate

`plan_study.md` has no growth rule. Its corpus grew ~94,000 production lines in
the 659 commits between `develop` and the branch the plan was written on.

**From the Year-2 portfolio decision (§6) onward: production line count may not
grow without a matching removal.** Measure it in every reserve week; record it in
the year-end review. New work lands as a *replacement*, not an addition.

This is the same principle as `plan_study.md` §4.5 — "a smaller project is a
better outcome than a fully read project" — stated as an enforceable invariant
rather than an aspiration. Without it, no weekly rate finishes.

## 6. The one structural change: cut the portfolio in Year 2, not Year 5

Even at 20 h/week the corrected scope does not fit in 520 weeks by rescoping alone.
The accounting:

| | Weeks |
|---|---|
| New material to place: `c-api` 20, `graphics-ext`/CNAEXT 20, `platform` 13, unclassified renderers 4 | **−57** |
| Recovered by folding standalone fix-weeks into Track B (§3) | **+60** |
| Reserve raised 4 → 6 weeks per year (§7) | **−20** |
| **Subtotal** | **−17** |
| Recovered by moving the portfolio decision from Y5 Q4 to Y2 Q4 | **+12** |
| **Balance** | **−5** (absorbed by reading slack, §1) |

So: **the plan fits in ten years at twenty hours a week if and only if the renderer
portfolio is cut in Year 2 instead of Year 5.** That is the single highest-leverage
change in this document, and it is not primarily a scheduling trick:

- Tier C is **58,371 lines**, plus its tests, examples, CI minutes and docs.
  Deciding it in Y2 removes it from *eight* subsequent years of audits, sanitizer
  passes, fidelity diffs, performance baselines and release matrices — not just
  from one reading week.
- `plan_study.md` already commits to this decision and already says the corpus
  should shrink. Making it late is what makes it expensive.
- It is the earliest decision that can be made honestly: it needs the `Graphics`
  public API (Year 2) and the capability-reporting audit (W63), **not** a deep
  read of each renderer. Deciding to delete something does not require having read it.

## 7. Sustaining twenty hours for ten years

Twenty hours a week for 520 weeks is two weekday evenings plus most of a weekend
day, without a gap, for a decade. The schedule is not the risk; attrition is.

- **Reserve rises from 4 to 6 weeks per year** (60 total, 1,200 h). Still never
  pulled forward. At 20 h/week the debt from a bad month is twice as large, so
  the buffer that absorbs it must be too.
- **A week is complete at one commit, not at twenty hours.** `plan_study.md` §1.2
  is right that a week without a commit is incomplete; the converse must also
  hold, or the plan becomes a timesheet.
- **The yearly review (`plan_study.md` §4.4) is binding, not ceremonial.** At the Year-1 reserve
  week, remeasure §0's table. If the corpus grew, §5 was violated and *that* is
  the year's finding.
- **Ten thousand hours is the deliberate-practice number, and its precondition is
  feedback, not time.** Blocks A2 and B2 — running it, measuring it — are that
  precondition. A year that quietly drops them is a year of reading, not practice.

## 8. Success criteria

`plan_study.md` §5 stands, with three additions that its own baseline made
unverifiable:

- The corpus is **smaller** than the 867,783 lines measured on 2026-08-30, and the
  reduction is deliberate and recorded.
- Every line of the T2 tier is covered by a generator or a round-trip test, and no
  human has read it line by line — because none needed to.
- The `AI-Assisted:` tally over ten years is under budget, and every line in it is
  covered by an assertion a human wrote.
